🔍 Wall Integrity Test

Sweeps a range of wall lengths and checks every brick course for gaps that aren't exactly one mortar joint wide — i.e. spots where mortar (or empty space) is filling a gap a brick should fill. Uses the app's own layout engine, so results reflect exactly what "Draw Wall" would produce. Your current inputs are restored and the wall is redrawn automatically once the test finishes. Testing every bond and brick type together can take a minute or two — narrow the length range or widen the step for a quicker check.
